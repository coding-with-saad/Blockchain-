## 🌟 Fungible Tokens (ERC-20)

* Identical: Every token is exactly the same.
* Interchangeable: One token can be swapped for another.
* Divisible: You can own a fraction of one.
* Example: Digital currencies or utility points.

------------------------------
## 🎨 Non-Fungible Tokens (NFTs)

* Unique: Each token has distinct metadata.
* Non-interchangeable: Cannot be swapped one-for-one.
* Indivisible: You must own the whole asset.
* Example: Digital art or virtual real estate.
